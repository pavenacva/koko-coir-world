How It Works

From dry disc to thriving seedling.
In three steps.

No soil mixing. No mess. No guesswork. Each compressed plug expands into a perfectly aerated home for one seed.

Step 1

Soak

Place a plug in warm water for 5–10 minutes. Watch it expand to 5× its size, fluffy and ready to plant.

Step 2

Sow

Drop one seed into the pre-formed hole. The fine coir fibers hold moisture and air in perfect balance.

Step 3

Transplant

Roots grow right through the plug — move the whole thing into soil or hydroponics. Zero transplant shock.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a plug take to expand?

About 5–10 minutes in warm water. The plug swells to roughly five times its compressed size and is ready to plant immediately.

Do I need to add nutrients?

Coir itself is inert, which makes it perfect for germination — seeds carry their own starter nutrition. Once your seedling has its first true leaves, begin feeding with a diluted liquid fertilizer or transplant into nutrient-rich soil.

Can I use Coco Plugs for hydroponics?

Yes — coir plugs are a hydroponics favorite. They fit standard net cups, hold moisture without waterlogging, and won't shed soil into your reservoir.

What are Cocochips and who are they for?

Cocochips are chunky coconut husk pieces used as a growing substrate for orchids, aroids, bromeliads, and other epiphytic plants. They provide exceptional drainage and airflow, keeping roots healthy and fungus-free. Available in 800g retail packs and 5kg bulk bags.

Is the Neem Oil ready to use?

Yes — our neem oil comes with emulsifier pre-mixed, so it dilutes directly in water with no dish soap needed. Simply mix with water at the recommended ratio and spray. It's organic-compliant and safe for use up to the day of harvest.
