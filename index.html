FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a plug take to expand?

About 5–10 minutes in warm water. The plug swells to roughly five times its compressed size and is ready to plant immediately.

Do I need to add nutrients?

Coir itself is inert, which makes it perfect for germination — seeds carry their own starter nutrition. Once your seedling has its first true leaves, begin feeding with a diluted liquid fertilizer or transplant into nutrient-rich soil.

Can I use these for hydroponics?

Yes — coir plugs are a hydroponics favorite. They fit standard net cups, hold moisture without waterlogging, and won't shed soil into your reservoir.

Are the plugs really 100% biodegradable?

Yes. KoKo plugs are pure coconut fiber with no plastic netting or synthetic binders. Transplant the whole plug — it breaks down naturally as roots grow through it.

Why coir instead of peat moss?

Peat is mined from wetland bogs that take millennia to form and store enormous amounts of carbon. Coir is a renewable byproduct of coconut harvesting. It also re-wets more easily, resists mold, and keeps a better air-to-water balance for roots.
